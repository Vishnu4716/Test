

1. WEEKLY KPI SUMMARY

Give me this week's production support KPI summary including FCR, MTTR,

ticket volume, re-route rate and escalation rate. Compare it with the

previous week and highlight significant changes.

2. WEEKLY ACTIVITY REPORT

Generate the agent weekly report showing ticket volume, FCR, MTTR,

re-routes, escalations and category distribution. Highlight the top

operational issues that require manager attention.

3. SKILL HEATMAP

Show the current skill-demand and skill-gap analysis. Identify which

technical skills are most frequently required by tickets and where the

available engineering capability may be insufficient.

4. RE-ROUTE ANALYSIS

Analyze tickets that were re-routed between teams. Identify the most

common categories causing re-routes, the teams involved and the likely

routing gaps based on the available ticket data.

5. COST AND ROI

Give me the month-to-date support ROI and cost-per-ticket analysis.

Break down the major drivers of support cost and identify areas where

automation or process improvement could reduce effort.

6. TICKET DISTRIBUTION

Show the current distribution of tickets by category, team and skill

requirement. Identify the categories generating the highest workload.

7. TICKET ANALYSIS

Analyze ticket {TICKET_ID}. Show its current status, category, assigned

team, required skills, routing history and any indicators that the ticket

may require manager attention.

8. TOP PROBLEM AREAS

Identify the top recurring ticket categories and problem areas during

the selected reporting period. Show ticket volume, impact on MTTR/FCR,

and the teams or skills most affected.

9. ESCALATION ANALYSIS

Analyze escalated tickets during the selected period. Identify the main

reasons for escalation, affected categories and teams, and any recurring

patterns.

10. PRODUCTIVITY ANALYSIS

Analyze support team productivity using available ticket data. Show

ticket volume, resolution performance, FCR, MTTR and workload distribution

across teams.

11. CATEGORY PERFORMANCE

Compare support performance across ticket categories. Show ticket volume,

FCR, MTTR, re-route rate and escalation rate for each category and

highlight areas requiring attention.

12. PROACTIVE RECOMMENDATIONS

Based on the latest available support KPIs and ticket trends, identify

the top three operational areas that require manager attention. Provide

evidence for each recommendation and suggest a practical next step.

13. MONTH-TO-DATE REPORT

Give me a month-to-date production support report covering ticket volume,

FCR, MTTR, re-route rate, escalation rate, skill demand, cost per ticket

and ROI. Compare against the previous available period where possible.

14. MANAGER WEEKLY REPORT

Prepare a concise management report covering:

- Overall ticket volume

- FCR

- MTTR

- Re-route rate

- Escalation rate

- Top ticket categories

- Skill demand and gaps

- Cost per ticket

- Key trends

- Top three recommended actions

Use only available data and clearly identify any unavailable metrics.
